About Matt K Collectibles

Matt K Collectibles is your destination for unique custom statues. We have previous completed projects available for sale, as well as commissions available if you have a specific idea in mind. Our mission is to provide high-quality, detailed models that bring your favorite characters and concepts to life.

  • Various mediums used, such as plastic kits, cast resin or even 3D printing.
  • Painted to a high standard for a premium finish.
  • High attention to detail and craftsmanship in every piece.
  • Custom requests are welcome to bring your vision to life.
